A little about us

Small batches.
Big-hearted baking.

We’re Petal & Crumb: a neighbourhood patisserie built around the belief that good days are often made of little things.

From our kitchen to your table

Made slowly.
Shared gladly.

Our shop began with a small oven, a handwritten recipe book, and a soft spot for the way a shared pastry can turn an ordinary afternoon around.

Today, we still bake in little batches, choose ingredients with care, and make room at the counter for one more. From celebration cakes to a just-because cookie, everything is made to bring a little sweetness to your day.

We’re glad you found us. Come by, say hello, and let us put the kettle on.
